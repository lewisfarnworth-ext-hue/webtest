Section 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Test Sales Tax Act 2026.

Section 2. Definitions

In this Act, unless the context requires otherwise:

  1. "Taxable sale" means a sale of tangible goods to a consumer in the Republic of Testland.
  2. "Tax rate" means the rate set out in Section 3.
  3. "Registered seller" means a person registered under Section 4.

Section 3. Rate of tax

A tax is imposed on every taxable sale at the rate of 5 per cent of the sale price.

Section 4. Registration

A seller whose taxable sales exceed 10,000 units of currency in a calendar year must register with the Testland Revenue Office within 30 days.
